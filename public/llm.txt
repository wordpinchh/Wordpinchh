# Wordpinchh — B2B Content Marketing & AEO Agency

## What We Do
Wordpinchh is a B2B content marketing and Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) agency based in Mumbai, India. We help Indian SaaS companies, tech startups, and professional service firms create content that ranks on Google and gets cited by AI platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Microsoft Copilot.

## Our Core Services
- Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) — structuring content to appear in AI-generated responses
- Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) — building visibility on Perplexity, ChatGPT, Copilot
- B2B Content Strategy — topical authority mapping, content pillar planning, buyer journey mapping
- Content Production — blog posts, case studies, whitepapers, and long-form B2B content
- Technical Content SEO — schema markup, structured data, site audit and remediation
- AI-Ready Content Systems — building repeatable content workflows for SaaS and tech companies
